### Project Overview
* **Topic:** Hydraulic Design and Optimization of a Bench-Scale Sedimentation Basin ($V = 23.46\text{ L}$).
* **Methodology:** Combined physical tracer stimulus-response experiments (using Methylene blue dye and FIJI/ImageJ optical intensity extraction) with 3D Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD) modeling in **ANSYS Fluent**.
* **Key Findings:** 
  * Validated the 3D finite-volume CFD model (773,744 tetrahedral cells) against physical datasets, achieving < 6% relative error in mean residence time and < 12% in peak exit time.
  * Demonstrated that installing an internal deflector baffle eliminates bottom short-circuiting, reducing stagnant dead zone volume by **77.4%** (from 25.64% down to 5.79%).
  * Quantified operational trade-offs, proving that increasing inlet velocity eliminates dead space but severely increases hydraulic dispersion (Morrill Index rising up to 7.71).
